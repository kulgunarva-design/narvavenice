Narva Venice

A place where every shore has a story

There is a place in Narva where the pace of the city gives way to the sound of water and unhurried conversations by the jetty. This is Kulgu, a corner of the Narva reservoir known as Narva Venice. Canals run between rows of boat garages, boats sway beside the banks, and small wooden walkways lead straight to the water.

Much of what you see here was made by the owners themselves. Garage doors, wooden decks and carefully tended places to relax all bear signs of someone’s work. Over time, a community grew around the boat moorings, bringing together people for whom Kulgu became an important part of life.

How it all began

The history of this place is closely connected to water and Narva’s industrial past. Long before the boat garages appeared, Kulgu had harbour quays through which goods were transported for the city and its businesses.

In the middle of the twentieth century, the creation of the Narva reservoir transformed the surrounding landscape. The new waters attracted people who enjoyed fishing and boating. Jetties, small wooden buildings and shelters for boats gradually appeared along the banks.

An important milestone came in 1969, when land was officially allocated for building boat garages. The Kulgu association began to develop. Buildings became more substantial, access to the water improved, and the rows of garages along the canals gradually took on the appearance we recognise today.

More than a place to keep a boat

People come to Kulgu for different reasons. Some prepare their tackle and go fishing; others repair a boat, make things or tend a small garden. Some spend weekends here with family, meet friends or simply relax beside the water.

Behind doors that look similar from the outside lie very different stories. For some, this place holds memories of their first boat trips with their parents. For others, it is somewhere they have spent years shaping with their own hands. Children have grown up here, new neighbours have arrived, and familiar routines have tied people to this shore.

Together, these things have given Kulgu its distinctive character: a place for boats, workshops, summer holidays and good neighbours.

Discovering Kulgu

The canals and unusual buildings attract travellers and photographers. From the bank, you can notice individual details; on a boat trip, you can see how they come together in the landscape: garages at the water’s edge, small jetties, boats and green shores.

Getting to know Kulgu also means getting to know its people. Conversations with local residents reveal how the place has changed, how the garages were built and what memories are connected to life beside the canals.

We want to tell the story of Narva Venice through everyday life, family memories and the gatherings that bring neighbours and visitors together.

Keeping our shared stories alive

Kulgu continues to change while keeping its connection to the past. Its history lives on in family photographs, the memories of long-time residents, and the boats and buildings cared for by successive generations.

We created this website to collect those memories, share the present-day life of Narva Venice and help people get to know one another. There is room here for an old photograph, a story about a neighbour, an account of working together and news of upcoming gatherings.

When visiting Kulgu, care for its peace, water and shores, and respect the owners’ personal space. If you have a story of your own connected to this place, share it with us.

Every shore has a story. Ours has a whole way of life.
